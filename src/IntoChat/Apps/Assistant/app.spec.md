# Assistant

Answers a question with its system prompt, using the model chosen in the "Model" setting.

## Scenario: The assistant answers with its system prompt

With the scripted model replying "Paris is the capital of France.", asking "What is the capital of
France?" answers exactly that reply, and the model was told to answer in at most three clear
sentences.

## Scenario: The assistant answers a real question (live)

With the default model, asking "What is the capital of France?" gives an answer that mentions
"Paris". Live scenarios document the app with real models; they are not part of the deterministic
gate.
