Still imperfect. The WHO mechanism is real and fully carried for the owner's own principals and the assistant acting on one of them. The scenario's two-person example sits in an undetermined space: the amended spec neither permits nor refuses a cross-principal connect, so the audit answers a question about an act whose legality is unstated. StatedIntent is the one forgeable half. The whole trail is trustworthy against product-surface actors only — cluster membership IS the authority, and there is no asymmetric signing. Pre-rail wires read provenance-absent forever, and tombstones accumulate in the single non-reentrant graph grain whose ConnectionsFrom is a full deserialize.
